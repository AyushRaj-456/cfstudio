# Privacy Policy for CF Studio

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

CF Studio ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data handling practices.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
CF Studio does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, Codeforces account credentials, code submissions, or analytics.

## 2. Local Device Storage
All user preferences (selected themes, custom 3-color palettes, background transparency, surface blur settings, and custom font family names) are stored exclusively on your local device using standard browser local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and local browser `IndexedDB`. No user data ever leaves your browser.

## 3. Permissions Used
- **`storage`**: Used solely to persist your visual customization settings locally on your computer.
- **Host permissions (`https://codeforces.com/*`, `https://*.codeforces.com/*`)**: Used exclusively to apply your chosen visual theme styles and background presentation to Codeforces pages. The extension never inspects, captures, or transmits any page content, user submissions, or private data.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Tracking
The extension contains:
- **Zero** third-party trackers, analytics, or telemetry scripts.
- **Zero** external advertising scripts.
- **Zero** background network communication with external servers.

## 5. Contact & Support
If you have questions or feedback regarding CF Studio, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
